Business Recommendations – Maven Fuzzy Factory

1. Improve Customer Retention
- Develop targeted email campaigns and remarketing strategies to encourage repeat purchases.
- Create a customer retention campaign within 30 days of the first purchase, considering the average 35.16-day time to second purchase.
- Segment customers based on purchase history, frequency, and product preferences.

2. Increase Repeat Purchases
- Introduce cross-selling and product bundling strategies to encourage customers to purchase additional products.
- Offer personalized product recommendations based on customers' previous purchases.
- Monitor repeat purchase rate and returning customer revenue monthly.

3. Optimize Marketing Performance
- Evaluate marketing channels based on conversion rate, revenue per session, and customer acquisition performance.
- Allocate more budget to campaigns delivering higher-quality traffic and profitable customer acquisition.
- Investigate underperforming campaigns and optimize landing pages and advertising strategies.

4. Improve Website Conversion
- Analyze the customer journey from landing page to purchase.
- Identify pages with high traffic but low conversion rates.
- Test landing page layouts, product descriptions, calls-to-action, and checkout improvements.

5. Reduce Refunds
- Investigate products and categories contributing to the highest refund volume and refund amount.
- Improve product descriptions, images, and customer expectations.
- Monitor refund rate and refund costs regularly.

6. Increase Average Order Value
- Introduce product bundles, complementary product recommendations, and upselling opportunities.
- Promote relevant add-on products during the shopping and checkout process.
- Track Average Order Value (AOV) to evaluate the effectiveness of these initiatives.

7. Establish KPI Monitoring
Track the following KPIs through the Power BI dashboards:

- Revenue
- Gross Profit
- Gross Margin
- Orders
- Conversion Rate
- Revenue per Session
- Average Order Value
- Repeat Purchase Rate
- Refund Rate
- Marketing Performance

## Expected Business Impact
These actions aim to improve customer retention, increase customer lifetime value, strengthen marketing efficiency, improve website conversion, reduce refund losses, and support sustainable revenue growth.
