Business Insights – Maven Fuzzy Factory

1. Revenue & Business Performance
- Generated $1.94M in revenue across 32,313 orders.
- Achieved a 62.74% gross margin, generating approximately $1.22M in gross profit.
- Average Order Value (AOV) was $59.99, indicating the average revenue generated per order.

2. Customer Insights
- Acquired 31,696 customers, with 31,105 one-time customers.
- Identified 591 repeat customers, highlighting an opportunity to improve customer retention and repeat purchases.
- Repeat purchase rate was 1.86%, indicating potential for stronger retention and customer lifecycle strategies.
- Average time to second purchase was 35.16 days.

3. Marketing Performance
- Generated 472,871 website sessions.
- Overall website conversion rate was 6.83%.
- Revenue per session was $4.10, providing a benchmark for evaluating traffic quality and marketing effectiveness.

4. Product & Order Insights
- Average items per order was 1.24.
- Order and revenue metrics provide a baseline to identify product performance, purchasing trends, and opportunities for cross-selling and upselling.

5. Refund & Loss Analysis
- Refund amount totaled $85,338.69.
- 1,731 items were refunded.
- Refund rate was 4.32%, highlighting the need to investigate product quality, customer expectations, and refund-driving products.

Key Business Takeaways
- Revenue and gross profit provide a strong foundation for measuring business performance.
- Customer retention represents an opportunity for growth, given the low repeat purchase rate.
- Website conversion and revenue-per-session metrics can help evaluate marketing efficiency.
- Refund analysis can help identify product and operational issues.
- Product bundling, cross-selling, and targeted marketing can potentially increase customer value and revenue.
