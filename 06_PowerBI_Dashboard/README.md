
Power BI Dashboard

This folder contains the interactive Power BI dashboard developed for the Maven Fuzzy Factory end-to-end business analytics project.

Dashboards

1. Executive Overview
Provides a high-level view of business performance, revenue, profitability, orders, customer metrics, and growth trends.

2. Revenue & Profitability Analysis
Analyzes revenue trends, gross profit, gross margin, product profitability, and overall business performance.

3. Marketing Performance
Evaluates marketing channels, campaigns, conversion rates, traffic sources, landing pages, device performance, and revenue contribution.

4. Product Performance
Analyzes product revenue, gross profit, margins, refund rates, product contribution, and monthly profitability trends.

5. Customer & Website Analytics
Explores customer acquisition, purchase behavior, repeat purchases, website conversion funnel, and customer cohort retention.

Advanced Power BI Features

- Interactive dashboards and cross-visual filtering
- Synchronized Year and Month slicers
- Dynamic dashboard titles
- Reset Report bookmarks
- Product drill-through analysis
- Marketing Campaign drill-through analysis
- Custom product tooltip
- KPI cards and business performance scorecards
- Time intelligence analysis

Tools Used

- Power BI Desktop
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Modeling
- Interactive Data Visualization
