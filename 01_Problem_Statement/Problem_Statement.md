Problem Statement

Project Overview

Maven Fuzzy Factory is an e-commerce business that sells products through its online website.

The business generates data from website sessions, pageviews, customer orders, product purchases, and refunds.

Business Problem

The company needs to understand its e-commerce performance and identify opportunities to improve revenue, customer experience, marketing effectiveness, and product performance.

Key Business Objectives

1. Analyze overall sales and revenue performance.
2. Evaluate customer purchasing behavior and repeat visits.
3. Measure marketing channel effectiveness and conversion rates.
4. Analyze website traffic and purchase funnel performance.
5. Evaluate product performance and refund trends.
6. Identify business opportunities and recommend data-driven actions.

Project Approach

The project follows an end-to-end analytics workflow using:

1. SQL Server for data cleaning, transformation, and analysis.
2. Python for exploratory data analysis and visualization.
3. Power BI for interactive dashboards and business reporting.


Expected Outcome

Deliver actionable business insights and recommendations that help improve e-commerce conversion, revenue growth, marketing efficiency, and customer experience.
